First goal: the Diamond Challenge

Come to a meeting by yourself or with friends. You'll leave with a team and an idea.

The Diamond Challenge is a worldwide startup competition for high school teams, run out of the University of Delaware. You pitch either a business idea or a social-impact idea, and we want to send teams from our school.

You turn in a 3–5 page write-up of your idea and a 60-second video. Every team needs an adult advisor, and Mrs. Priebe is ours.

The deadline is 5 PM Eastern on a school day, so we're aiming to have everything submitted by our January 7 meeting.

What you'll learn

How a business actually gets started, in the order you'd do it for real.

  1. Find a problem worth solving

    Start by noticing what annoys people. Every good business fixes a real problem.

  2. Talk to real customers

    Figure out who has the problem and whether they'd pay to make it go away.

  3. Make the money work

    Pricing, costs, and whether you'd actually turn a profit.

  4. Pitch it

    Tell your idea in 60 seconds, then in five pages. That's exactly what the Diamond Challenge asks for.

Questions

Do I need a business idea already?

Nope. Most people show up without one. We brainstorm together, and you'll leave your first meeting with a team and an idea.

Do I have to be in BPA?

No, anyone can join. If you're already in BPA, this fits right alongside it.

Does it cost anything?

No. The club is free, the food is on us, and the Diamond Challenge is free to enter.

I don't have a team. Is that a problem?

Not at all. Teams are 2 to 4 people. Come by yourself and we'll help you find one.

Can I join after the first meeting?

Yes, come any Thursday. The Diamond Challenge is due January 14, so the sooner you start, the better your shot.
