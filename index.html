Questions

Do I need a business idea already?

Nope. Most people show up without one. We brainstorm together, and you'll leave your first meeting with a team and an idea.

Do I have to be in BPA?

No, anyone at Mayo can join. If you're already in BPA, this fits right alongside it.

Does it cost anything?

No. The club is free, the food is on us, and every competition we're entering is free.

I don't have a team. Is that a problem?

Not at all. Come by yourself and we'll help you find one. Most competitions take teams of 2 to 5, and a few let you enter solo.

Can I join after the first meeting?

Yes, come any Thursday. The sooner you start, the more competitions you can enter this year.

Who runs this?

I'm Gates. I started the club, and Mrs. Priebe is our faculty advisor. Email me with any questions.
